INDUSTRIAL WORKPLACE SAFETY SOP

Document: General Industrial Workplace Safety Procedures
Version: 1.0

1. MOBILE PHONE USAGE

Mobile phone use is prohibited while a worker is actively performing
industrial work or operating machinery, unless the phone is required for
an authorized work task.

If unauthorized mobile phone use is observed:

1. Stop the unsafe activity when it is safe to do so.
2. Inform the worker that personal mobile phone use is not permitted during
   active work.
3. If the behavior continues or creates an immediate safety concern, notify
   the responsible supervisor.
4. Record the incident with the available visual evidence and timestamp.

Required incident information:
- Worker tracking ID
- Incident timestamp
- Visual evidence
- Camera identifier
- Relevant work area or machine

2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

Workers must use the PPE required for their assigned work area and task.

PPE requirements should only be considered violated when the required PPE
and its absence can be established from reliable information.

If a PPE non-compliance incident is confirmed:

1. Notify the worker when safe to do so.
2. Request correction of the PPE issue.
3. Escalate repeated or serious violations to the supervisor.
4. Record the incident and supporting evidence.

3. UNSAFE MACHINE INTERACTION

Workers must not interact with industrial machinery in a manner that creates
an unsafe condition.

If unsafe machine interaction is confirmed:

1. Stop the unsafe activity when it is safe to do so.
2. Keep the worker clear of the hazardous area.
3. Notify the responsible supervisor.
4. Record the incident with visual evidence.

For machine-related incidents, include:
- Machine or work area
- Worker tracking ID
- Timestamp
- Camera identifier
- Visual evidence

4. UNSAFE POSITION

A worker may be considered to be in an unsafe position when their physical
position is clearly exposed to a hazardous condition.

If an unsafe position is confirmed:

1. Alert the worker when safe to do so.
2. Move the worker away from the hazardous position if appropriate.
3. Notify the supervisor for serious or repeated conditions.
4. Record the incident and available evidence.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING

Every confirmed safety incident should contain:

- Incident type
- Worker tracking ID
- Timestamp
- Camera identifier
- Machine or work area when relevant
- Observed evidence
- Applicable SOP guidance
- Recommended immediate action

The incident report must distinguish between directly observed evidence and
assumptions. Information that cannot be visually established should not be
presented as fact.
